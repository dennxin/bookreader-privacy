Common questions

Where are my books and bookmarks stored?
Everything stays on your device. We do not have access to your library.

I deleted the app. Can I get my data back?
Deleting the app removes its data from your device. If you had an iCloud or device backup from before you deleted it, restoring that backup may bring the data back.
