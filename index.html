BookReader App Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Overview

BookReader App does not collect, store, or share any personal information. This policy explains what that means for you.

BookReader App ("the app") is developed by Mỹ Nguyễn ("we", "us"). This policy applies to the BookReader App for iPhone and iPad, available on the Apple App Store.

Information we do not collect

We do not collect any data from you through the app. Specifically, we do not collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details
  • Your location
  • Your reading history, books, notes, or bookmarks
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage data, analytics, or crash reports
  • Payment or financial information

We do not track you across other apps or websites, and we do not show third-party ads.

Data stored on your device

Any books, reading progress, bookmarks, or settings you create stay on your device. We cannot see or access them. If you use iCloud backup, that data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. Deleting the app removes its data from your device.

Third-party services

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. Apple may collect some information when you download the app from the App Store, as described in Apple's own privacy policy.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If we ever start collecting data, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before that version is released.
